OTPGrabber

Copy one-time passcodes from Gmail to your clipboard automatically.

What it does

OTPGrabber is a Firefox extension that scans your Gmail inbox for messages containing one-time passcodes, detects the code, and copies the newest one to your clipboard. Optionally, it can auto-fill codes into supported one-time-code fields.

Privacy

OTPGrabber runs entirely in your browser. Your Gmail access is limited by your own Google account and OAuth session. Codes and settings are stored locally in your browser's extension storage. No data is sent to the developer or any third party. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Get started

Install the extension from Mozilla Add-ons, connect your Gmail account in the options page, and you're ready to go. All scanning and processing happens on your device.
