Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

OTPGrabber is a Firefox extension that helps you copy one-time passcodes from your Gmail inbox to your clipboard. This policy explains what information is accessed and how it is used.

Information we access

  • Gmail messages: With your explicit consent, the extension uses the Gmail API via OAuth 2.0 to search for and read messages in your Gmail account for the purpose of detecting one-time passcodes. Only messages needed to find codes are read.
  • Google account info: Basic profile information (email address) may be used to display which account is connected.

How your information is used

  • All processing happens locally in your browser. Detected codes are copied to your clipboard on your device.
  • OAuth tokens, settings, and a short history of detected codes are stored in browser.storage.local on your device.
  • The extension does not send your emails, codes, or personal data to the developer or any third-party servers.
  • The only external servers contacted are Google's own services (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, gmail.googleapis.com) as required to authenticate and retrieve your messages.

Data sharing and retention

  • No sharing: Your data is never sold, shared, or transmitted to anyone other than Google as part of the normal Gmail API and OAuth flow you explicitly authorize.
  • Local retention: Data remains in your browser until you clear it (by disconnecting the account, clearing extension data, or uninstalling the extension).

Permissions

OTPGrabber requests only the permissions necessary for its functionality: identity, storage, alarms, clipboardWrite, notifications, and host permissions for Google's OAuth and Gmail endpoints. An optional host permission (<all_urls>) is only used if you enable the optional auto-fill feature, and even then it only fills one-time-code fields you focus.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
